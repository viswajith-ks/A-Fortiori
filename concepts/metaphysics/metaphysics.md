# Metaphysics

**Category:** metaphysics

## Definition
Metaphysics is the branch of philosophy that studies the fundamental nature of reality, existence, and the basic categories of being.

## Description
Metaphysics asks the most general questions about what exists and what it means to exist. While science studies particular aspects of reality, metaphysics examines the underlying structure that makes scientific inquiry possible. It investigates concepts like substance, causation, time, space, possibility, and necessity.

## Historical Development

### Ancient Metaphysics
- **Aristotle**: First systematic metaphysics; study of "being qua being"
- **Substance theory**: Primary substances (individuals) and secondary substances (universals)
- **Four causes**: Material, formal, efficient, final ([Aristotelian Causation](../meta-philosophy/aristotelian-causation.md))
- **Potentiality and actuality**: Framework for understanding change

### Medieval Metaphysics
- **Aquinas**: Integration of Aristotelian metaphysics with Christian theology
- **Essence and existence**: Distinction between what something is and that it is
- **Divine simplicity**: God as pure actuality without potentiality
- **Analogy of being**: Different modes of existence

### Modern Metaphysics
- **Descartes**: Substance dualism (mind and matter as distinct substances)
- **Spinoza**: Substance monism (one substance with infinite attributes)
- **Leibniz**: Monadology (reality composed of simple, windowless substances)
- **Kant**: Transcendental idealism; phenomena vs noumena

### Contemporary Metaphysics
- **Analytic tradition**: Precise analysis using formal logic
- **Possible worlds semantics**: Modal logic and counterfactuals
- **Philosophy of science**: Metaphysical implications of physics
- **Experimental philosophy**: Empirical investigation of metaphysical intuitions

## Core Areas

### Ontology
**The study of what exists**
- **Categories of being**: Objects, properties, relations, events
- **Abstract vs concrete**: Numbers vs tables
- **Universal vs particular**: Properties vs instances
- **Existence vs being**: What does it mean to exist?

### Identity and Change
**What makes something the same over time?**
- **Numerical identity**: A = A
- **Qualitative identity**: Exact similarity
- **Personal identity**: What makes you "you" over time?
- **Ship of Theseus**: If all parts are replaced, is it the same ship?

### Causation
**What is the nature of causal relationships?**
- **Efficient causation**: Event A causes event B
- **Causal powers**: Objects have dispositions to cause effects
- **Counterfactual analysis**: A causes B if B wouldn't occur without A
- **Probabilistic causation**: Causes raise probability of effects

### Time and Space
**What is the nature of temporal and spatial reality?**
- **Absolute vs relational**: Newton vs Leibniz
- **A-theory vs B-theory**: Temporal becoming vs eternal relations
- **Presentism vs eternalism**: Only now exists vs all times equally real
- **Spatial dimensionality**: Why three dimensions?

### Modality
**The study of possibility and necessity**
- **Logical necessity**: Truths of logic and mathematics
- **Metaphysical necessity**: Essential properties and natural kinds
- **Nomological necessity**: Laws of nature
- **Epistemic possibility**: Consistent with what we know

### Properties and Relations
**What are properties and how do they relate to objects?**
- **Universals vs tropes**: Shared properties vs particular property instances
- **Intrinsic vs extrinsic**: Properties objects have independently vs relationally
- **Natural vs artificial**: Properties that "carve nature at the joints"
- **Structural universals**: Properties defined by relations

## Methods in Metaphysics

### Conceptual Analysis
- **Analyzing concepts** to reveal necessary truths
- **Thought experiments**: Possible scenarios that test intuitions
- **Modal arguments**: What's possible/necessary/impossible

### Scientific Metaphysics
- **Taking science seriously** as guide to reality's structure
- **Naturalistic approach**: Metaphysics continuous with science
- **Special relativity**: Implications for simultaneity and time
- **Quantum mechanics**: Challenges to classical concepts

### Transcendental Arguments
- **Starting from undeniable features** of experience or thought
- **Reasoning to necessary conditions** for their possibility
- **Kantian strategy**: What must be true for experience to be possible?

### Formal Methods
- **Modal logic**: Systematic study of necessity and possibility
- **Mereology**: Logic of parts and wholes
- **Set theory**: Mathematical foundations for abstract objects

## Relationship to Other Areas

### Epistemology
- **How can we know** metaphysical truths? ([Knowledge](../epistemology/knowledge.md))
- **A priori vs empirical**: Reason vs experience in metaphysics
- **Skeptical challenges**: Can metaphysics be objective? ([Objectivity](../epistemology/objectivity.md))

### Philosophy of Science
- **What does science** tell us about reality's fundamental structure?
- **Theoretical entities**: Do electrons, quarks, fields really exist?
- **Laws of nature**: Are they descriptive or prescriptive?

### Philosophy of Mind
- **Consciousness and physical reality**: Hard problem of consciousness
- **Mental causation**: How can mental states cause physical events?
- **Personal identity**: What makes psychological continuity possible?

### Ethics
- **Moral realism**: Do moral facts exist independently of attitudes?
- **Free will**: Are we causally determined or genuinely free?
- **Value in nature**: Is there objective value in reality itself?

## Contemporary Debates

### Grounding
- **What makes truths true?** Distinction between truth and truthmakers
- **Fundamental vs derivative**: Some facts may be more basic than others
- **Explanatory hierarchy**: Levels of metaphysical explanation

### Composition
- **When do parts compose** a whole? (Special composition question)
- **Mereological nihilism**: Only simples exist, no composite objects
- **Universalism**: Any collection of objects composes something

### Material Constitution
- **Statue and clay**: Are they identical or distinct?
- **Essential vs accidental**: Which properties are necessary?
- **Constitution vs identity**: Different relations between objects

## Challenges to Metaphysics

### Logical Positivism
- **Metaphysical statements** are meaningless (unverifiable)
- **Only analytic or empirical** statements have cognitive content
- **Response**: More sophisticated understanding of meaning and verification

### Quinean Critique
- **No sharp distinction** between analytic and synthetic truths
- **Ontological relativity**: What exists is relative to conceptual schemes
- **Naturalized metaphysics**: Continuous with science, not prior to it

### Experimental Philosophy
- **Cultural variation** in metaphysical intuitions
- **Demographic effects**: Gender, culture affect philosophical judgments
- **Challenge to method**: Are intuitions reliable guides to truth?
