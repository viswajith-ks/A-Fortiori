# Proof

**Category:** logic

## Definition
A proof is a logically valid sequence of statements beginning with axioms or premises and leading to a conclusion, demonstrating the truth of that conclusion.

## Types
- **Mathematical proof:** Formalized structure using axioms and inference rules.
- **Philosophical proof:** More informal or dialectical, aiming for persuasive argument.

## Properties
- Valid steps at each line.
- Derives conclusion from premises without logical gaps.
- Often relies on axioms, [deduction](deduction.md), and accepted [inference](inference.md) forms.
